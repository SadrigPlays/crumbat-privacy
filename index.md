CrumBat Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 26, 2026

CrumBat is a Twitch Extension that lets viewers participate in bat-catching hunts and collect rewards.

Information we process

CrumBat may process Twitch-provided identifiers to recognize participation and determine who catches a bat first. If you choose to share your identity through Twitch's identity-linking feature, your Twitch user ID may be processed.

How information is used

Information is used to record hunt results, identify winners, and support collectible rewards. We do not sell personal information.

Storage

Hunt results and identifiers may be stored in the database used to operate CrumBat to maintain results and rewards.

Your choice

You may decline Twitch identity sharing. Features that require identifying your Twitch account may then be unavailable.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the creator through the Twitch channel where CrumBat is offered.
